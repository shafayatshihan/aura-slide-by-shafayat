Tell Aura-Slide about your presentation

Only 4 questions are required (marked *). Skip anything else and Claude will decide.

Step 1

What kind of presentation is it?

Pick the closest one.

Example: Solar-powered water pump for rural irrigation

Example: EEE 400 Thesis, or ICME 2026

Step 2

Who is presenting?

These go on the title slide and the last slide.

Example: Dr. Jane Rahman

Example: Professor, Dept. of EEE

Step 3

Who is watching, and for how long?

This decides how simple the words are and how many slides you get.

Example: 10

Step 4

Tell us about your work

Short answers are fine. Your report files will fill in the details.

Example: fluid mechanics, molecular dynamics, power electronics, statistics, microbiology

Example: We built a low-cost sensor that measures soil moisture and sends it to a phone.

Step 5

What should each slide cover?

Optional. Leave it on and Claude plans the slides for you.

Write one line per slide. You can pick a file from your folders to show on that slide.

Step 6

Your files

Put your report, pictures and data into the folders, then press Check again. Claude reads everything here.

Step 7

Anything special on the slides?

Tick what you need.

Step 8

On presentation day

So your slides work on the day.

Step 9

Anything else?

Last step. All optional.

Example: no jokes, do not show our raw data

Last check

Does this look right?

Press Save. You can come back and change it any time.
