Deck mark

Start with the object on the right: say what it is and what is going wrong with it, before any number.

Walk the three numbers top to bottom and give each one its source out loud: who measured it, and when. If asked where a number comes from, name the paper or report; never round it up to sound bigger.

Land on the last line: this is the gap the rest of the talk fills.
