# Headspace — Brand Style

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Warm, optimistic and round. Pure white pages, big friendly headlines, and a cast of smiling blob characters in a
bright multi-colour palette built around the signature orange. Light theme.

## Colors
| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#ffffff` | `#f9f4f2` warm off-white for soft sections |
| Primary | `#ff7300` | smiley orange (character faces, big shapes; never small text: 2.9:1 on white) |
| Multi-accent set | `#ffce00` gold · `#ffa500` amber · `#ffa1cc` candy pink · `#3b197f` meditative purple · `#27455c` teal-navy | used together in illustrations |
| Text Primary | `#2d2c2b` | warm charcoal, never pure black |
| Text Muted | `#44423f` | body copy |
| Border | `#e2ded9` | |

**Color scheme:** light
**Accent rule:** multi-accent is the brand. Keep text charcoal; let colour live in characters, pills and underlines.

## Typography
- **Display + Body:** Headspace Apercu (Colophon, licensed), with Apercu Pro Mono for labels.
  Aura blend: **Quicksand** 700 display (OFL, rounded like the smile) + **DM Sans** 500 text (OFL) + **Reno Mono** kicker (CC BY 4.0).
| Role | Size |
|---|---|
| H1 | 112 px, 700, −0.02em |
| Kicker | 28 px mono, uppercase, in a gold pill |
| Body | 36 px, 500 |

## Spacing & Shape
- Base grid: 8 px
- Border radius: pills `9999px`; everything else round (circles, blobs)
- Shadows: none
- Framework hint: custom

## Voice & Personality
- Tone: kind, reassuring, lightly playful
- Energy: medium
- Audience: anyone looking after their mind

## Illustration
Smiling characters built from circles and soft blobs: closed happy eyes (∩ arcs), simple smile, flat fills from the
multi-accent set, a coloured ground ellipse. No outlines, no gradients.
